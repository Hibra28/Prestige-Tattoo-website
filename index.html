      01 — About

      A studio built around custom artistry.

      Prestige Inks Tattoo Studio is a custom-focused tattoo studio in Canada. We design personalised tattoos across a range of styles — shaped around your vision, your placement and your personal expression.

      Our approach centres on creativity, precision and collaboration. From first idea to final linework, we take time to understand what you want to express, refine the concept with you, and craft a piece that feels intentional and uniquely yours. Client-focused, detail-driven and quality-obsessed — that is the Prestige Inks experience.
